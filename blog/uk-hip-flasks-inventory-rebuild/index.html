How We Rebuilt an Inventory System for a Client Who Isn't Technical

AC
Al Camasis
Founder, Altos · August 12, 2026 · 4 min read

The first time we spoke with Mark, he said something we hear more often than you would think. Almost before anything else, he told us he needed someone with real senior experience for this, because he is not a technical person. He runs UK Hip Flasks, he knows his business inside out, and he did not want to be handed jargon and decisions he had no way to judge.

That one sentence shaped the whole project. And honestly, it is our favorite kind of brief.

The real work was the conversation

When a client is not technical, the hardest part is not the code. It is making sure the person paying for the work always understands what is happening, in plain language, without ever feeling talked down to. So we explained everything at a high level, and we backed it up with mockups: simple, visual versions of the screens he could react to instead of imagining from a description. Whenever there was a real decision, we laid out the options, told him what we would recommend and why, and left the choice with him. Suggesting is not the same as forcing. Our job was to make the path clear, not to push him down it.

Making the right calls for him

Mark told us what he wanted for his business. He did not tell us how to build it, because that is not his world. So a lot of the important decisions were ours to make, and we made them the way a professional should: choosing the option that gives the best result, even the parts he will never notice. A lot of good engineering is invisible, and when someone trusts you with those calls, you make them as if the business were your own. We also kept spotting improvements along the way and brought every one to him, but never built anything without a final yes first. Proactive, never presumptuous. Mark later said the suggestions were always well thought out, which is exactly the bar we hold them to.

Fast, tested, and clean

AI is a real part of how we work now, and it made the build fast and precise. It took the tedious weight off the process so we could spend our time on judgment and quality, which meant a steady stream of releases that just worked. We focused the testing where it mattered most, the connections to his webstore, because if stock and orders do not sync correctly the whole thing loses its value. And we held the code to a standard the whole way through. In Mark's own words, the new system is noticeably faster and cleaner than what he had before.

The result

The system went live, and Mark put it better than we could:

“The replacement system is a great improvement on previous versions: faster, cleaner, and it just works better overall. I have found them reliable and responsive.”

Mark Nogula, UK Hip Flasks

That is the whole job, really. Understand what a non-technical owner actually needs, make the right calls on his behalf, and hand back something faster, cleaner, and more reliable than what he had. If you have something you need built and you are not even sure how to describe the technical side, that is the part we are here for. Tell us what you want, and we will take it from there.
