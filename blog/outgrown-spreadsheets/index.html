5 Signs Your Business Has Outgrown Its Spreadsheets

AC
Al Camasis
Founder, Altos · October 29, 2026 · 3 min read

Almost every business we work with started on spreadsheets, and that is not a mistake. Spreadsheets are fast, flexible, and free. The problem is that they never tell you when you have outgrown them. You find out slowly, one late night and one costly mistake at a time.

The five signs

  • The same data lives in more than one place. Stock in one sheet, orders in another, a copy in someone's inbox. Every change has to be made three times, and sooner or later one of them is wrong.
  • Only one person really understands it. If one colleague guards the master spreadsheet and nobody else dares touch the formulas, your business has a single point of failure. When they are on holiday, things quietly stop.
  • You copy and paste between systems every day. Orders exported from the webstore, pasted into a sheet, then into accounting. Every manual step is slow, and every one is a chance for an error.
  • Customers find your mistakes before you do. Overselling something you no longer have, or shipping the wrong quantity. When customers discover your data is wrong, the spreadsheet has stopped keeping up.
  • Simple questions take an afternoon to answer. "What sold best last month?" "What do we need to reorder?" If every answer means filters and pivot tables, you are deciding with less information than you should.

What to do about it

Outgrowing spreadsheets does not mean you need a huge, expensive system. Usually it means one well-built tool that keeps your data in one place and connects to what you already use, like your webstore and accounting, so information flows on its own instead of by copy and paste. Start with the part that hurts most, and grow from there. If you are wondering what that costs, we broke it down in What Does Custom Software Actually Cost?

If two or more of these sounded familiar, tell us what you are juggling, and we will tell you honestly whether it is time.
